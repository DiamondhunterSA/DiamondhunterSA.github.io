A little about me

I'm DiamondhunterSA. This is my small corner of the web: a place for projects, learning notes, and paths that begin with a question.

I like the atmosphere of Dark Souls and Elden Ring: places that feel weathered, mysterious, and full of paths waiting to be found. This chronicle carries a little of that mood into a place for writing.

What lives here

The Forge is for projects and the decisions behind them. The Archives is for learning notes and the details worth keeping. Beyond the Fog is for curiosity that has not chosen a destination yet.

A chronicle in progress

This is a new beginning. The first learning and exploration entries are labeled Starter entry; they demonstrate the shape of the journal while personal writing finds its place.
